The FORUS Sport vision

The people behind sport
should share in its future.

Sport brings people together. A cooperative approach can help that connection create lasting value for the communities behind it.

FORUS Sport brings together a vision for stronger clubs, more visible players, community media and partnerships that support grassroots development.

We are developing the network and its country plans. This site introduces the vision and opportunities to get involved.
